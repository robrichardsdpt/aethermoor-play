AETHERMOOR

The Shattered Light

Before the first dawn, the Architects crossed the dark between stars, carrying the First Light — the captive heart of their dead home star — and grew a world around it. Something followed them across the void. When the Spire broke in the Sundering, the Light burst into seven shards and the world tore itself into endless wandering lands. The Architects are mountains now. Their monoliths still whisper.

You are the last Wayfarer of the Last Hearth. Walk the broken world. Find the seven Shards. Relight the dark — before what followed them finds it first.

The Unlit roam the far lands, and a Guardian coils around every Shard. Defeat them in the Umbra — the folded nothing the Architects once sailed — and each victory will teach you its Arts, until the Wayfarer is something far greater.
